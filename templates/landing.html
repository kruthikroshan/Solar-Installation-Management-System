Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the PM Surya Ghar government subsidy work?

For residential rooftop installations, the government provides direct financial assistance up to ₹78,000 for systems up to 3 kW. Our team assists with discom inspection and net-metering paperwork to get the subsidy credited directly to your bank account.

What is the difference between On-Grid and Off-Grid solar?

On-Grid systems remain connected to the electrical utility grid and feed surplus energy back via net-metering (reducing your electric bill to near zero). Off-Grid systems include a dedicated solar battery bank that provides complete autonomy during blackout hours.

How do I claim a warranty if a component stops working?

Simply use our public Warranty Checker and enter the serial number printed on your panel or inverter. If the component is within the warranty window, a free replacement ticket is automatically assigned to our service engineers.

How long does the entire installation process take?

Once your site survey is approved and advance payment is recorded, warehouse dispatch and on-site rooftop mounting are completed in under 48 hours.
