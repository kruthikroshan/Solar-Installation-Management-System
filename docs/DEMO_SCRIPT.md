# 6-minute demo script

1. Public form (/enquiry): submit a 3 kW On-Grid enquiry as a customer. It lands with sales instantly and cannot vanish.
2. Sales (sales@): open the new lead, record the call (30 sq m, bill 4000, grid OK), mark Qualified. Try a quote: blocked. Book a survey.
3. Survey technician (survey@): fill roof direction, tilt, cable distance, approve.
4. Sales: create the quotation from the "On-Grid 3 kW" template and show the parts list that appeared by itself. Confirm the order with a 50% advance and pick an installer.
5. Warehouse (warehouse@): job is in "To pack". Show the pick list, try a wrong serial (error), enter the right serials (or scan QR labels), dispatch, mark delivered: warranty starts.
6. Installer (installer@): the job is already on the schedule. Complete it with times, power test, photo, signature.
7. Service (service@): search "Ramesh" (seeded 30-month-old system). Inverter shows Expired; resolve and show the automatic invoice. Then an in-warranty customer: free repair with replacement serial.
8. Accountant (accountant@): balance payment, subsidy status, technician pay, profit per job.
9. Director (director@): dashboard, red loss-making job (Green Valley Foods), unfinished jobs, open repairs, create the read-only share link.
